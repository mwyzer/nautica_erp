# STCW Convention & 2010 Manila Amendments
## International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers

## Section A-VIII/1: Fitness for Duty & Mandatory Rest Hours

### 1. Mandatory Rest Periods for Watchkeeping Personnel
All persons who are assigned duty as officer in charge of a watch or as a rating forming part of a watch, and those whose duties involve designated safety, prevention of pollution and security duties, shall be provided with a rest period of not less than:
1. Minimum Rest per 24-hour period: A minimum of 10 (ten) hours of rest in any 24-hour period.
2. Minimum Rest per 7-day period: 77 (seventy-seven) hours in any 7-day period.
3. Division of Rest Hours: The hours of rest may be divided into no more than two periods, one of which shall be at least 6 (six) hours in length, and the interval between consecutive periods of rest shall not exceed 14 (fourteen) hours.

### 2. Exceptions & Emergency Operations
Muster drills, firefighting drills, and emergency lifeboat operations shall be conducted in a manner that minimizes the disturbance of rest periods and does not induce fatigue. If regular rest hours are interrupted by calls to work, an adequate compensatory rest period must be provided immediately.

## Chapter II & III: Minimum Safe Manning & Mandatory Certifications

### 1. Mandatory Bridge & Engine Command Roles
To obtain official Port Clearance approval, every commercial vessel must be manned in accordance with the Minimum Safe Manning Document (MSMD) issued by the Flag State Administration:
- Master (Nakhoda): Must hold a valid STCW Certificate of Competency (COC Class I Deck / ANT-I) and valid Medical Fitness Certificate.
- Chief Engineer (KKM): Must hold a valid STCW Certificate of Competency (COC Class I Engine / ATT-I).
- GMDSS Radio Operator: At least one designated deck officer holding a General Operator's Certificate (GOC) for maritime distress communications.

### 2. Certification Expiration Clause
No seafarer is permitted to sail if their statutory STCW Certificate of Competency (COC) or Basic Safety Training (BST) expires prior to the scheduled date of arrival at the port of destination. Expired certifications invalidate vessel legal manning and cause immediate Port State Control detention.
