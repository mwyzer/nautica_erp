# MARPOL Annex VI: Prevention of Air Pollution from Ships

## Regulation 14: Sulphur Oxides (SOx) and Particulate Matter Limits

### 1. Global Sulphur Cap (IMO 2020 Standard)
The sulphur content of any fuel oil used on board ships operating globally outside Emission Control Areas (ECA) shall not exceed 0.50% m/m (mass by mass), typically supplied as Very Low Sulphur Fuel Oil (VLSFO).

### 2. Emission Control Areas (ECA / SECA Limits)
Within designated Emission Control Areas (such as the Baltic Sea, North Sea, North American ECA, and regulated regional ports including Singapore Port Limits and Malacca Strait green zones):
- The sulphur content of fuel oil used on board shall not exceed 0.10% m/m (Ultra Low Sulphur Fuel Oil - ULSFO or Marine Gas Oil - MGO).
- Ships utilizing multi-fuel tanks must complete fuel changeover procedures prior to entering ECA waters, recording fuel changeover volume, time, and coordinates in the official ship logbook.

## Regulation 18: Fuel Oil Quality, Bunker Delivery Notes (BDN) & Sampling

### 1. Bunker Delivery Note (BDN) Mandatory Retention
1. Details of fuel oil for combustion purposes delivered to and used on board shall be recorded by means of a Bunker Delivery Note (BDN).
2. The BDN must be retained on board in such a place as to be readily available for inspection at all times for a statutory period of not less than 3 (three) years following fuel bunkering.
3. The BDN must state the vessel's IMO number, port of bunkering, date, fuel density, viscosity, and explicit sulphur content declaration (% m/m).

### 2. MARPOL Representative Fuel Samples
1. The BDN shall be accompanied by a representative sample of the fuel oil delivered (MARPOL Sample), sealed and signed by the supplier's representative and the ship's chief engineer.
2. The representative sample shall be retained on board under the ship's control until the fuel oil is substantially consumed, but in any case for a period of not less than 12 (twelve) months from the date of delivery.
