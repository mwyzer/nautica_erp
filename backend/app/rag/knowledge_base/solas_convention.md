# SOLAS Convention (International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea)
## Chapter II-1: Construction - Machinery and Electrical Installations

### Regulation 26: General Machinery Requirements
All main and auxiliary machinery essential for propulsion and the safety of the ship shall be provided with effective means of control and operation. Main propulsion systems and vital auxiliary engines must be maintained in seaworthy condition without critical defects that could impair vessel maneuverability.

### Regulation 29: Steering Gear Seaworthiness Requirements
1. Ships must be provided with a main steering gear and an auxiliary steering gear to the satisfaction of the Administration.
2. The main steering gear and rudder stock shall be of adequate strength and capable of steering the ship at maximum ahead service speed.
3. Hydraulic systems operating the steering gear actuator must be free from critical leakage or high-pressure seal failure. Any severe hydraulic actuator leakage represents a Chapter II-1 deficiency prohibiting port departure until surveyor certification.

## Chapter V: Safety of Navigation

### Regulation 34: Safe Navigation & Passage Planning
1. Prior to proceeding to sea, the master shall ensure that the intended voyage has been planned using appropriate nautical charts and nautical publications.
2. The passage plan shall identify a route that takes into account:
   - Any relevant ships' routing systems.
   - Sufficient sea room for the safe passage of the ship throughout the voyage.
   - Known navigational hazards and adverse weather conditions.
3. Statutory Safe Fuel Margin: The master and chief engineer shall verify that the vessel carries adequate bunker fuel on board (Remaining On Board - ROB) for the intended steaming voyage plus a minimum statutory safety reserve margin of at least 15% (or 3 steaming days under bad weather contingency) to reach the port of refuge or destination safely.
